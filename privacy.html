Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2025

1. Introduction

Tails ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Account Information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Account type (buyer or breeder)

Location Information:

  • Current GPS location (with your permission)
  • City and postcode
  • Country (Australia)

Listing Information (for breeders):

  • Pet photos and descriptions
  • Pet details (breed, age, price, etc.)
  • Breeder bio and contact information

Usage Information:

  • Search queries
  • Browsing history within the app
  • Favorite listings
  • Messages sent and received
  • Analytics data (app interactions, crashes, performance)

Device Information:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Mobile network information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect breeders with potential buyers
  • Display pet listings based on your location
  • Facilitate messaging between users
  • Send important notifications (messages, listing updates)
  • Verify breeder accounts through email verification
  • Improve and optimize the app experience
  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)

4. Location Data

We use your location data to:

  • Show you pets available near you
  • Auto-populate location fields when creating listings
  • Calculate distances between you and pet listings
  • Filter search results by proximity

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Denying location access will limit some features but you can still browse all listings.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information.

We may share your information with:

Other Users:

  • Your public profile information (name, photo, bio) is visible to other users
  • Breeders can see inquiries and messages you send
  • Buyers can see public breeder information and listings

Service Providers:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and hosting
  • Analytics providers for app performance monitoring
  • Push notification services

Legal Requirements:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Tails, our users, or others
  • To prevent fraud or security threats

We do not share your contact information (email, phone) with other users without your explicit consent.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text
  • Data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Firebase security rules prevent unauthorized data access

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Control location permissions
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, use the Privacy & Security settings in the app or contact us through the Help & Support section.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Necessary to provide our services
  • Required by law

When you delete your account:

  • Your personal information is permanently deleted
  • Pet listings you created are removed
  • Messages may be retained for compliance purposes
  • Analytics data may be retained in anonymized form

9. Children's Privacy

Tails is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will delete their account and information immediately.

10. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information:

Firebase (Google):

  • Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore database
  • Cloud Storage
  • Analytics
  • Crashlytics

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Australia, including the United States (where Firebase servers are located). These countries may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through:

  • In-app notification
  • Email notification
  • Updated "Last Updated" date

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Australian Privacy Principles

Tails complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with these principles.

14. No Payment Information Collected

IMPORTANT: Tails does NOT collect, store, or process any payment or financial information.

  • We do NOT collect credit card, debit card, or bank account information
  • We do NOT process any payments or financial transactions
  • All payment arrangements are made directly between users outside our platform
  • We have no access to or knowledge of any financial transactions between users

Any payment information shared between users is done entirely outside our platform and at users' own risk.

15. Your Consent

By using Tails, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or adjusting your privacy settings.

You acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy and accept all risks associated with sharing information through the platform.
