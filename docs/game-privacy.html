Tails: The Game

Privacy Policy · Last updated 9 October 2026

Tails: The Game (“the game”) is an iPhone game made by Tails Australia Pty Ltd. We built it to be fun and private. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The Tails marketplace has its own privacy policy.

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There’s no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

Your game data

Your pets, coins, items and settings are saved only on your device. We can’t see them, and they aren’t sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the game, this data is deleted too.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the game only which items you’ve bought, so it can unlock them.

Notifications, Live Activities and widgets

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are off unless you turn them on. The Dynamic Island, Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets read your pet’s details from the game’s storage on your device. You can turn any of these off in the game’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

Links to Tails Australia

The game includes optional links to tailsaustralia.com.au, which open in your web browser and are covered by the marketplace’s privacy policy.

Children

The game doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are made through Apple and can be controlled by parents with Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
